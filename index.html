How does the compatibility match score work?
Our match algorithm cross-evaluates your verified assessment proficiencies, declared projects, and career preferences against live industry skill matrices.
Is this platform free for college students?
Yes! Students have full access to profile management, career exploration, skill tests, and roadmap tracking without paywalls.
Can I print or export my resume?
Yes! Use the built-in Resume Builder page and press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) to generate a clean, monochrome paper-ready resume handled by our dedicated print stylesheet.
How does the CSS-only theme switcher function?
CareerNova utilizes modern CSS pseudo-classes and CSS variables, allowing immediate toggling between Light and Dark mode directly within your browser without JavaScript.
