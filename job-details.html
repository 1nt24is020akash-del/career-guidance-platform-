About Synthetix AI & The Role

Synthetix AI develops autonomous evaluation and fine-tuning pipelines for enterprise multimodal models. Backed by top venture funds, we are building the developer infrastructure necessary for high-reliability AI deployment at scale.

As a Junior ML Engineer, you will directly work alongside senior research scientists and systems engineers to optimize PyTorch inference runtimes, build automated dataset cleansing pipelines, and run LoRA adapter training experiments on GPU clusters.

Key Responsibilities

  • ✓ Construct and maintain distributed tokenization and vector indexing pipelines in Python and FastAPI.
  • ✓ Fine-tune open-weights foundation models (Llama, Gemma, Mistral) utilizing parameter-efficient methods (PEFT / QLoRA).
  • ✓ Implement automated evaluation benchmarks to detect model degradation, hallucinations, and latency regressions.
  • ✓ Collaborate with product teams to translate customer domain knowledge into high-quality benchmark datasets.

Required Qualifications

  • • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or equivalent coursework / portfolio.
  • • Demonstrated fluency in Python, NumPy, and PyTorch (verified via GitHub repositories or coursework capstone).
  • • Solid understanding of linear algebra, optimization algorithms, and transformer self-attention mechanisms.
  • • Familiarity with containerization (Docker) and Git version control workflows.
Bonus Points:

Experience deploying models via vLLM or Triton Inference Server, contributions to open-source Hugging Face libraries, or familiarity with CUDA kernel fundamentals.
