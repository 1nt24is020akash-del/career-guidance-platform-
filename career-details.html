Role Overview & Market Need

Modern AI Engineers operate at the intersection of mathematical reasoning and distributed systems software engineering. Companies rely on AI Engineers to fine-tune open weights, orchestrate vector databases, construct low-latency embeddings, and ensure robust guardrails against hallucinations.

Typical Responsibilities

    Design & Train Deep Learning Architectures: Construct PyTorch / JAX neural models for vision, speech, tabular, or NLP domain tasks.
  • ✓
    Build Production Retrieval Pipelines (RAG): Connect foundation language models with hybrid vector databases and document chunkers.
  • ✓
    MLOps, Quantization & Serving: Optimize models via FP16/INT4 quantization, TensorRT, vLLM, and autoscaling clusters.
